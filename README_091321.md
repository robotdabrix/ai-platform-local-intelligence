# AI Platform and Local Intelligence

> Intelligence that helps people do more with what they know.

## The Opportunity

AIWBT brings intelligent assistance into tools for learning, creating, building, and exploring ideas. Its platform connects those experiences so people can use helpful intelligence in the moments it matters.

## Who It Serves

AI developers and product teams building with local or hosted model services.

## What It Makes Possible

- Make intelligent assistance available across connected products
- Help people explore questions and work through complex ideas
- Support practical creative, learning, and developer experiences

## How People Use It

1. Choose an AIWBT experience suited to your goal.
2. Describe what you are trying to understand or accomplish.
3. Review the assistance and decide how to use it.

## How It Connects

- Supports AIWBT products such as Local Dev Agent, Intelligentsia, and Advanced Content Engine, and can connect to other participating products through ConnectWBT.
